Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

ClipForge runs on your own computer. We do not operate servers that receive your data, and we do not sell or share your information.

Information ClipForge uses

When you connect a TikTok account and approve access, ClipForge receives:

  • Your basic profile: display name, username and profile picture (user.info.basic, user.info.profile).
  • Permission to upload videos you choose to your TikTok inbox or account (video.upload, video.publish).
  • Access and refresh tokens that let ClipForge make those uploads for you.

Connected YouTube and Instagram accounts work the same way, with the permissions you approve on those platforms.

How it is used

  • Your name and picture are shown in ClipForge so you can see which account you are posting to.
  • Tokens are used only to upload the clips you post or schedule.
  • ClipForge does not read your followers, messages, likes or other videos.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on your computer. Tokens are stored locally and are sent only to the platform they belong to (for example, TikTok tokens go only to TikTok's servers). Videos are sent only to the accounts you choose to post to.

Deleting your data

Disconnect an account in ClipForge's Accounts screen to delete its stored tokens and profile details from your computer. You can also revoke ClipForge's access from your TikTok app settings under Security and permissions. Uninstalling ClipForge removes all of its local data.

Children

ClipForge is not intended for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age to use TikTok in their country.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
